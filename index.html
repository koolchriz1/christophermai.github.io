Grasslands: Tiny World

A collaborative Unity VR experience seen from the perspective of a miniaturized human.

Grasslands: Tiny World is a collaborative virtual reality project built in Unity in 2026. The experience places the player in the perspective of a miniaturized human, making scale the central idea of the project. It brings my portfolio into an immersive format, where the viewer takes on a role inside a virtual world.

A different sense of scale

The premise starts with a change in perspective: experiencing a world as a tiny person. That relationship between the player and the environment is the starting point for this VR experience.
